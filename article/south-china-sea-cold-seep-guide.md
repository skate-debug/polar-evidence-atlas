# South China Sea cold-seep collection / 南海冷泉文献专题

Release: 12 October 2026. Publication window: 12 October 2016–12 October 2026.

The Earth & Ocean Evidence Atlas now contains an SCS cold-seep collection alongside its polar collection. It prioritizes microorganisms and includes environmental measurements that help interpret microbial ecology. The first inventory combines Europe PMC and Crossref searches and contains 260 records, of which 34 have curated evidence profiles. Five records are explicitly labeled preprints. The remaining 226 entries have provisional title/abstract tags and require source review before they can support graph claims. The complete [APA 7 bibliography](../references/cold-seep-literature-apa7.md) and [CSV inventory](../dist/cold-seep-literature.csv) are downloadable.

地球与海洋证据图谱在原有极地专题之外，新增南海冷泉专题。首批目录整合 Europe PMC 和 Crossref 检索，收录 260 条文献记录，其中 34 篇已建立证据档案，5 条明确标记为预印本。其余 226 条记录的分类来自标题与摘要的初步识别，须核查原文后才能用于知识图谱论断。研究重点为微生物，同时保留解释微生物生态所需的环境与地球化学背景。

## Sampling compartments / 采样介质

| Category | 中文 | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Water column | 水体 | Absolute water depth, hydrographic layer, free-living/particle-attached fractions, filters and volumes |
| Bottom water and plumes | 底层水与羽流 | Height above seabed, distance from vent, dissolved methane and plume position |
| Sediments and porewater | 沉积物与孔隙水 | Centimeters/meters below seabed, core identity, SMTZ position, porewater chemistry |
| Sediment–water interface | 沉积物–水界面 | Operational sampling position, oxygen/redox context, water versus sediment material |
| Host-associated microbes | 宿主相关微生物 | Host species, tissue, symbiont/epibiont distinction, recovery and preservation history |
| Carbonates and microbial mats | 碳酸盐与微生物席 | Substrate, activity state, biomarker versus contemporary molecular evidence |

Use separate fields for water depth and sediment depth. “Bottom water” is also not a uniform height above seabed: Li et al. (2021) sampled approximately 50 cm above Haima seabed, whereas Zhong et al. (2023) analyzed water collected 160–200 m above seabed. These studies belong in the same broad compartment but are not directly equivalent interface samples. [Li et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2021.753952), [Zhong et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/microorganisms11123001)

水深、距海底高度和沉积物深度应分别记录。底层水并不对应固定采样高度，不能将距海底几十厘米和数百米的样品直接当作相同界面样品。

## Research themes / 研究主题

Fourteen overlapping themes are available: community diversity and assembly; metagenomes, MAGs, and evolution; methane cycling; sulfur cycling and syntrophy; nitrogen cycling; phosphorus and trace elements; organic matter and carbon fixation; viruses and microbial defenses; protists and fungi; chemosynthetic symbioses; cultivation and microbial resources; geochemistry and methane transport; pollution and environmental change; and sampling, sensors, and observatories.

十四类主题分别为：群落多样性与组装；宏基因组、MAG 与进化；甲烷循环；硫循环与互营；氮循环；磷与微量元素；有机质与碳固定；病毒与微生物防御；原生生物与真菌；化能合成共生；培养与微生物资源；地球化学与甲烷输运；污染与环境变化；采样、传感器与观测站。一篇论文可归入多个主题，分类计数不能直接相加作为论文总量。

Site filters include Haima, Site F/Formosa Ridge, Haiyang 4, Dongsha/GMGS2, Shenhu, Qiongdongnan, Lingshui, Wenhai, and the newborn seep. A regional/unspecified-site class and a cross-region comparison class prevent assigning unsupported station locations. Hydrate-bearing subseafloor studies retain their habitat identity rather than being treated as surface active-seep studies.

## Useful entry studies / 建议起读文献

- Water communities and size fractions: Zhang et al. (2020), [particle-attached and free-living prokaryotes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpolbul.2020.111230).
- Reusable environmental genomes: Zhang et al. (2022), [23 metagenomes and 768 MAGs](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01586-x), including water, fluids, and sediments.
- Water-column metabolic structure: Liu et al. (2025), [Site F MAGs and metabolic models](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08471-4), and Zhai et al. (2026), [vertical and horizontal water-column variation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00248-026-02722-5).
- Sediment methane and assembly: Niu et al. (2023), [methane-supply regimes](https://doi.org/10.1111/mec.16786), and Zhang et al. (2023), [fermentation in deep hydrate sediments](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-023-01482-5).
- Interface sulfur and nitrogen: Sun et al. (2023), [Campylobacterota physiology and in situ evidence](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.00117-23).
- Fungi and microbial domains: Shekarriz et al. (2023), [fungal networks and transcripts](https://doi.org/10.1128/spectrum.01978-22), and Xu et al. (2025), [paired DNA/RNA diversity](https://doi.org/10.1093/ismeco/ycaf002).
- Viral ecology: Peng et al. (2023), [Haima viral evolutionary patterns](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41396-023-01491-0), and Han et al. (2026), [defense and anti-defense systems](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-68174-6).
- Cross-compartment ecology: Zhu et al. (2026), [Haima water and sediment communities](https://doi.org/10.1128/spectrum.00256-26).
- New ecological developments: Liang et al. (2026), [multi-year newborn-seep biofilter development](https://doi.org/10.1093/nsr/nwag266); Sui et al. (2026), [nitrogen-linked methane oxidation and N₂O](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.01446-26); and Lan et al. (2026), [in situ clam–symbiont responses](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aed8728).

These entries provide different kinds of evidence. Genome annotations describe metabolic potential, RNA provides expression or activity indicators, co-occurrence networks represent associations, and incubations measure rates under their experimental conditions. Each curated profile records the appropriate citation scope and what the paper cannot support. Global catalog totals remain separate from SCS-specific counts.

基因注释、RNA 信号、共现网络、培养实验和原位测量的证据强度与解释范围不同。引用时应先查看证据档案的研究设计、适用范围、证据位置和局限性，避免将基因潜力写成实测通量，将统计关联写成直接互作，或将全球数据目录的总量写成南海特有数量。

## Updating / 持续更新

```bash
make update-cold-seeps
python3 scripts/export_cold_seep_references.py --verify
make translations
make all
make public-snapshot
make public-check
```

The discovery window moves with the execution date unless `--since` and `--until` are supplied. DOI/title deduplication and Crossref preprint links help avoid counting an available published version twice. New discoveries remain candidates until a curator extracts source-supported findings, sampling scope, limitations, and evidence locators. Public snapshots omit copied abstracts; complete working caches remain local.

Publication dates retain their reported precision. For month/year-only records,
the complete possible date interval must fall within the ten-year window.
Boundary-overlapping records need an exact date before inclusion. This prevents
assigning an invented day or silently including future issue dates.

`config/collections.json` defines the bilingual browsing taxonomy. `data/sources.json` and `data/claims.json` store reviewed evidence. `data/public/cold_seep_candidates.json` stores the public discovery inventory. The collection can be extended without changing existing source IDs or the public website URL.
