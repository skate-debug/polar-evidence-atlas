# Cold-seep discussion comparisons / 冷泉讨论比较指南

Updated 13 October 2026. Publication window: 13 October 2016–13 October 2026.

This collection supplies literature comparisons for a South China Sea (SCS) cold-seep paper. It contains 49 inspected comparison profiles: 45 newly added sources and four studies already represented in the SCS collection. Shared DOIs have one source record in the atlas. Twenty-six profiles are limited to abstracts; 23 use inspected full-text passages, including reused verification records. The international discovery inventory contains 461 DOI records, with 356 awaiting source review, 55 awaiting publication-version checks, and one withdrawn preprint excluded from graph evidence.

## Selecting comparisons / 选择比较

The paper's own Results have not been supplied. The table therefore identifies comparisons to select after the observed pattern, sample material and assay are known. Match water depth, height above seabed, sediment depth, particle fraction, tissue, seep-fluid composition, redox setting and sampling period. Publication year and sample collection year are recorded separately in profiles when available.

目前尚未提供论文自身的结果。下表用于在明确观测模式、采样介质与分析方法后选择比较文献。应对齐水深、距海底高度、沉积物深度、粒径、组织、冷泉流体组成、氧化还原条件及采样时期。档案在信息可得时分别记录发表年份与采样年份。

## Citation boundaries / 引用边界

An annotated gene supports metabolic potential. Transcripts and proteins add expression evidence. Isotope incorporation, controlled incubations and field measurements support different outcomes under their stated conditions. The profiles identify those conditions and record cases where a title's wording is broader than the Methods. Use the primary paper, not the atlas, as the scientific citation.

注释基因支持代谢潜力，转录本及蛋白质补充表达证据。同位素掺入、受控培养及现场测量在各自明确条件下支持不同结果。档案记录这些条件，并标注标题措辞比方法更宽泛的情况。科学引用应指向原始论文。

Compare a documented finding with the closest habitat and assay. If the data are metagenomic, use a rate study to explain a tested mechanism, while retaining the distinction between the mechanism measured elsewhere and the potential observed in the current samples. For regional differences, first consider matching depth, substrate, environmental context and processing. The [cross-basin prediction study](https://doi.org/10.1128/spectrum.03033-24) provides a concrete example of limited transfer across regions.

应将已记录的发现与最接近的生境和实验比较。若当前数据为宏基因组，可借助速率研究解释已检验机制，但须区分其他体系的实测机制与当前样品的功能潜力。讨论区域差异时，应先对齐深度、底物、环境背景及处理条件；跨海盆预测研究给出了地域迁移受限的具体实例。

Terrestrial ice/groundwater, the organic-rich Congo fan and Guaymas hydrothermal sediment comparisons are explicitly labeled as habitat or mechanistic context in the profiles. Their experimental or ecological contribution is retained without treating them as identical deep-sea cold-seep samples.

陆地冰／地下水、富有机质刚果深海扇及瓜伊马斯热液沉积物，在档案中明确标为生境或机制背景比较。保留其实验或生态贡献，同时说明与深海冷泉样品的环境差异。
