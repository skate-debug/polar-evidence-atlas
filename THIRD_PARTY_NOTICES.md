## Scholarly metadata

- DOI metadata is retrieved using DOI content negotiation and is commonly
  supplied by Crossref: https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/
- Candidate discovery metadata is retrieved through the Europe PMC REST API:
  https://europepmc.org/RestfulWebService
- Europe PMC explains that reuse of article material remains subject to the
  copyright holder's terms: https://dev.europepmc.org/Copyright

The committed files under `data/public/` retain only bibliographic and
discovery fields needed to reproduce the atlas. Abstract and full-text fields
are deliberately removed. All scientific works should be cited through their
DOI or other original identifier.
